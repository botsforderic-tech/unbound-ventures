June 27 to July 3, 2027|France, Italy, Switzerland

Tour du Mont Blanc

A fully supported 100 mile ultra across the Alps. Ten athletes, four staff and one loop around Mont Blanc through three countries.

The Haute Route, run

Dawn starts and high route variants keep every stage ahead of the day hiker crowds. Col de la Seigne before the tour groups, the Col Sapin high variant to Bonatti, Champex at first light.

Day 1

  • Chamonix to Les Contamines
  • Col du Tricot high variant

Day 2

  • Les Contamines to Courmayeur
  • Col des Fours and Col de la Seigne into Italy

Day 3

  • Courmayeur to La Fouly
  • 24 miles, 9,500 ft of climbing
  • Mont de la Saxe ridge and Grand Col Ferret into Switzerland

Day 4

  • La Fouly to Trient
  • 24 miles, 6,200 ft of climbing
  • Champex Lac at first light, then Fenêtre d'Arpette, the highest pass on the route

Day 5

  • Trient to Chamonix, finish
  • 19 miles, 4,600 ft of climbing
  • Col de Balme into France and the Grand Balcon to the finish
  • Finish line celebration dinner

March 21 to 25, 2027|Roatán, Honduras

Roatán Freediving

The next Unbound Ventures expedition. Explore crystal clear depths on the Great Mayan Reef using only the power of your breath.

Master freediving on one of the world's most pristine reefs

Five days of AIDA certified freediving training, plus Unbound breathwork, recovery and mindset sessions. A tropical reset at the end of winter.

Day 1

  • Exploration breathwork and mobility session
  • Welcome ceremony and dinner with Unbound coaches and the crew

Day 2

  • Unbound breathwork and functional movement session
  • Pool training: static apnea and safety fundamentals
  • First open water dives, 10 to 15 meters
  • Guest speaker on freediving mindset and breath control

Day 3

  • Open water line diving intro
  • First reef dive
  • Special guest speaker

Day 4

  • Dive among the sharks of Roatán's reefs
  • Final max line dives and rescue skills training

Day 5

  • Explore the island of Roatán
  • Final Unbound performance session
  • Expedition debrief and AIDA 2 certification
  • Closing ceremony

Expedition Leader

Eric Botsford

Eric has spent more than 15 years building some of the biggest names in endurance and functional fitness, from Tough Mudder and Ragnar Relay to leading the US launch of HYROX.

Before that he fought wildfire as a Helitack firefighter with the U.S. Forest Service and as an engine and aviation fireman with CAL FIRE, where long days carrying heavy loads in steep country were the job.

He is also a trained EMT with a background in wilderness medicine, so the person setting your pace is the same person ready to care for you when you are far from help.

He has guided expeditions with XPT and Wodventures, owned and coached at Good Times CrossFit, and today leads growth for WZA Sports. Unbound Ventures is where all of it comes together: real objectives, small crews and a leader who has carried the load you are about to carry.
